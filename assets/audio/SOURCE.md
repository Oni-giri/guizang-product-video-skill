# Audio examples and built-in SFX sources

- `score-example-keys.py`: the score from an early 48-second CodePilot promo (D major; chord pad, short-note melody, light drums). Depends only on the Python standard library and FFmpeg.
- `score-example-cinematic.py`: the score from the 50-second CodePilot film (F minor; supersaw pad, pluck arpeggios, sidechained drum kit, risers, impacts and convolution reverb). Depends on numpy/scipy/soundfile.
- Both examples are generated from waveforms, noise, envelopes and note arrangement, with no external samples and no generation-model calls. They are adaptation references in two different moods, not a fixed score for every product: borrow the synthesis and arrangement techniques, and re-derive key, tempo, instrumentation and section timing for each new film.
- `sfx/*.wav`: generated by this skill's `scripts/make_sfx.py`, 48 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM. Eleven sounds: click, click-alt, pop, toggle, typing, ding-dong, success, error, resolve, whoosh and sweep; durations and SHA-256 hashes are in `manifest.json`. No third-party recordings or reference-video samples.
- These original sound effects ship with the skill at the user's request, for promo films to use and adjust when no suitable sample can be found; they are not the Pixabay recordings used in the original film. Their source notes do not extend to externally downloaded samples and do not change the license attached to the components in `assets/fallback/`.
- This directory contains neither the original Pixabay recordings nor the original film's master that mixed them in. For the original recordings' sources and how to find them, see [score creation and SFX sourcing](../../references/audio-sourcing.md).

When using them, first find suitable sounds for each on-screen role and copy only what is missing; to change the timbre, regenerate into a new directory. After combining samples with music, still listen, adjust gain, align to actions, and duck the music smoothly when a sound effect plays.
